Current search result text reports 150 campuses (95 public + 55 private); because source pages and counts can change, the durable acceptance requirement is to capture dated source output through governed retrieval, not hardcode this count.
- **HEC recognition / affiliation scope** — official HEC Accreditation Section describes recognized universities/DAIs, recognized campuses, and recognized affiliated colleges of public-sector HEIs: https://www.hec.gov.pk/Site/Accreditation/default.aspx. This supports HEC as an authority for its defined recognition/affiliation scope, not for all school, TVET or professional-provider facts.
- **NAVTTC partner institutes** — official directory currently exposes 2,317 partner institutes, five listed regions and 141 districts, with visible counts Punjab 954, Sindh 354, KPK 358, Balochistan 183, and ICT 468: https://navttc.gov.pk/partner-institutes/. This official directory's displayed regional coverage does not establish AJK or GB completeness; regional TVET and boards need separately governed sources.
- **NAVTTC qualification-awarding-body scope** — official affiliation/accreditation pages list boards/regulators by province, including PBTE, SBTE, KP-BT&CE, trade-testing boards and AJK-related bodies: https://navttc.gov.pk/affiliation/ and https://navttc.gov.pk/wing/accreditation-and-certification/. Use these pages to discover the appropriate TVET authority and affiliation paths; do not treat them as a universal register of all schools/colleges.
- **IBCC qualification-awarding bodies** — official IBCC page lists recognized QABs, boards and schools: https://ibcc.edu.pk/qualification-awarding-bodies/. It explicitly cautions that recognition of a QAB does not automatically make every qualification it offers recognized for equivalence. The pipeline must preserve that distinction.
