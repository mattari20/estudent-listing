# MOD-025 — Institutes Directory: Moderation, Community Safety and Abuse Governance

**Document ID:** MOD-025
**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Final candidate — pending merge into `main`
**Owner:** eStudent.pk
**Created:** 2026-09-23
**Depends On:** MOD-015, MOD-017, MOD-018, MOD-024

## 1. Purpose
Govern community reviews, ratings, recommendations, corrections and abuse handling.

## 2. Community Content
User-generated content remains distinct from canonical institutional information.

## 3. Moderation States
Future states may include pending, published, limited, hidden, rejected, appealed, removed and restored.

## 4. Manipulation
Controls should address spam, duplicates, automation, coordinated manipulation, review bombing, self-promotion, competitor attacks, impersonation and retaliation.

## 5. Factual Claims
Community claims do not become canonical facts without independent evidence and verification.

## 6. Institution Responses
Institutions may respond through governed channels but must not silently edit independent community content.

## 7. Appeals
Future moderation should provide appropriate review and appeal mechanisms.

## 8. Consistency
Moderation rules should apply consistently to positive and negative feedback.

## 9. AI Moderation
AI may assist classification, spam/duplicate detection and prioritization, but must not fabricate evidence or silently suppress legitimate criticism.

## 10. Privacy
Moderation should minimize unnecessary exposure of contributor identity.

## 11. Ranking Interaction
Only eligible governed community signals may enter future aggregations; moderation status must not directly determine institutional rank.

## 12. Audit
Material moderation actions should be auditable.

## 13. Non-Approval of Implementation
No moderation vendor, AI model or automated enforcement technology is selected here.
