## 1. Purpose
Defines long-term security, privacy and access principles.

## 2. Data Minimization
Collect and retain only information necessary for approved purposes.

## 3. Public and Internal Data
Canonical public facts, internal evidence, audit information and personal contributor data remain distinct.

## 4. Contributor Privacy
Student, teacher, alumni and other contributor information must be protected; public display is limited to appropriate information.

## 5. Consent and Choice
Personal-data collection and optional personalization require appropriate notice and user choice.

## 6. Access Control
Administrative, moderation, verification and data-management capabilities require controlled access.

## 7. Write Operations
Public submissions must pass appropriate validation, moderation and verification before becoming canonical facts.

## 8. Auditability
Material changes and privileged operations should be auditable without unnecessary exposure of private information.

## 9. Abuse Prevention
Future controls should address spam, fraud, automation, credential abuse and rating manipulation.

## 10. Sensitive Information
Do not publish unnecessary personal, confidential or security-sensitive information.

## 11. Location Data
Campus locations may be public when appropriate; sensitive exact-location information requires separate controls.

## 12. Retention
Retention should reflect legal, operational, historical and privacy requirements.

## 13. Third Parties
Partner and API access requires explicit scopes and permissions.

## 14. AI and Privacy
AI systems may process only data they are authorized to receive.

## 15. Incident Governance
Future implementation should support detection, containment, investigation, recovery and applicable notification.

## 16. Non-Approval of Implementation
No authentication provider, encryption library, hosting model, security platform or compliance framework is selected here.
