## Guardrails

- Keep ambiguous identity, award title, or campus mapping in review/blocked status.
- Historical failure events are not necessarily unique unresolved rows.
- A source URL alone is not verification; capture the claim it supports.
- This template does not authorize edits, promotions, approvals, migrations, merges, or deletions in production.
