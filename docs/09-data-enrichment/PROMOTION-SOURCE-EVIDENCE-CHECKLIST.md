# Source evidence checklist for enrichment promotion

Use this checklist before classifying a candidate as eligible for promotion. It is a review aid only; it does not approve, promote, activate, or publish any record.

## Evidence requirements

For each candidate, capture the source identifier, source type, source URL or internal reference, retrieval timestamp, verification timestamp, and the specific claim supported. Prefer official institution, campus, department, admissions, or regulator sources for identity and programme facts.

Confirm that evidence supports the exact entity and claim:
- institution identity and official name;
- campus identity, address, and geography;
- programme title, award/level, discipline, and owning institution;
- offering delivery mode, campus association, and current status;
- intake label and application dates, if applicable.

A source that confirms the institution exists does not automatically verify its campuses, programmes, offerings, or intake dates.

## Freshness and conflict review

- Record when the source was retrieved and last verified.
- Treat expired application windows as historical evidence, not proof of current availability.
- Record conflicting sources separately; do not silently choose one.
- Mark inaccessible, stale, or ambiguous sources as unresolved.
- Preserve evidence IDs and safe summaries, not raw secrets or unrelated personal data.

## Promotion decision

Classify each claim as verified, partially verified, contradicted, stale, unavailable, or unknown. Keep a candidate blocked when a required claim is unknown or contradicted. Identity conflicts and source conflicts require human review.

## Audit output

The restricted report should include candidate/batch IDs, claim type, evidence IDs, verification status, freshness, conflict status, decision rationale, and next action. Public reports should not expose raw payloads, credentials, or unrestricted exceptions.

Any live mutation requires a separate reviewed impact plan and explicit authorization.
