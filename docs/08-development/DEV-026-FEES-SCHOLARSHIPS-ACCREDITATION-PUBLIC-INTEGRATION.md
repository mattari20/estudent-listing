# DEV-026 — Fees, Scholarships and Accreditation Public Integration

Status: Implemented

## Implemented
- Fee records are displayed in their program-offering context.
- Missing fee amounts remain explicitly non-blocking.
- Scholarship records are displayed separately from fees.
- Scholarship benefit and eligibility descriptions remain source-governed data.
- Accreditation/recognition records are displayed with their recorded status and authority.
- Rejected and withdrawn records are excluded from public presentation.

## Governance boundary
Fees and scholarships are optional. Unknown does not mean zero, unavailable, or not offered. Accreditation/recognition data is presented as recorded information and is not independently reclassified by the UI.
